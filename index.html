Matteo El Hariry

  • Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Luxembourg
  • CTO & Co-founder, NaubotiX

Hello! I’m Matteo, a Postdoctoral Researcher in Reinforcement Learning and Autonomous Robotics at the University of Luxembourg. I completed my PhD in December 2025, with a thesis on "Deep Reinforcement Learning Control for Autonomous Robots Mobility in Highly Uncertain Environments."

Alongside my research, I am CTO and co-founder of NaubotiX, a company developing onboard autonomy software for robots operating off-world.

My research develops reinforcement learning (RL) methods that let complex autonomous robots, from spacecraft and floating platforms to fixed-wing aerial vehicles, operate reliably under significant uncertainty and environmental disturbances.

Previously, I worked for 3+ years as a Machine Learning Engineer at Argotec, an aerospace company specializing in deep-space micro-satellites. I contributed to the core algorithms for the autonomous visual navigation of LICIACube and ArgoMoon, satellites that were part of NASA’s DART and Artemis I missions.

I hold a Master’s in Computer Science and Engineering (AI track) from Politecnico di Milano and a double BS degree from PoliMi and Tongji University. Outside research, I mentor refugees in Luxembourg through the RYSE Association.

I'm motivated by building and understanding AI systems that approach decision-making in ways new to humans, and that could help tackle humanity’s most pressing problems.
