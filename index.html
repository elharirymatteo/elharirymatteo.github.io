Matteo El Hariry

  • Postdoctoral Researcher · University of Luxembourg
  • CTO & Co-founder · NaubotiX

I develop simulators and reinforcement learning controllers for complex autonomous robots: satellites, wing-in-ground aircraft, wheeled rovers and more. PhD from the University of Luxembourg (2025); previously ML Engineer at Argotec, on autonomous navigation for LICIACube (NASA DART) and ArgoMoon (NASA Artemis I).

Research & Projects

ICE2THRUST 2025–present

RL-based autonomous rendezvous and docking for an EIC Pathfinder demonstrator that turns water ice into thrust.

Lead Research Scientist · Shown: SCoCaT, CoRL 2026

AIRSHIP FALCON-S 2023–present

RL flight control and a high-fidelity ground-effect simulator for unmanned wing-in-ground vehicles.

Lead Research Scientist · Horizon Europe

RoboRAN 2025

One IsaacLab framework for RL navigation across robots, tasks and media, with sim-to-real transfer.

TMLR 2025 · Project website ↗

DRIFT 2024

IROS 2024 (oral)

REACT 2021
